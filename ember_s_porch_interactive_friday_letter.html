Friday, October 2

A quiet week. The fire's still lit.

Nothing went out this week, Alex. I'm not going to dress that up. But look at something with me.

Look back down the road. Every light is an application from August and early September, at least thirty of them. A quiet week doesn't put a single one out.

Tap any light. Tap this card to go on.

Two no's came in, from Geotab and Loblaw. Here's what I noticed: you applied to them on August 6 and August 17. They took seven weeks and six weeks to answer.

So the road behind you is still being read. Silence on the rest isn't an answer yet.

And one window is lit up ahead. Last Thursday you talked with Maria at Randstad about a contract role, and you were back in your résumé within the hour. As far as I can see, that one's still open.

Here's what I can't see: what this week was. Rest? Or work that doesn't leave a trail, like portfolio pieces or calls? I'd rather ask you than guess.

Either way, the fire's here.

—Ember

P.S. If you were working on something I can't see, tell me in any chat and it goes in next Friday's letter.

Prototype: a world made for a week with nothing sent. No walking and no new lanterns; you stay by the fire and the letter looks back, then asks. Nothing from after October 2 appears. Facts are from your inbox, calendar and Drive (names and dates only); "at least thirty" is a minimum from confirmation emails.
